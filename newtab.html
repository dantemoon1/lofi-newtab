What's New in v1.2.0

YouTube Playlist Support

You can now paste a YouTube playlist link to loop through multiple videos instead of playing just one.

Background Pomodoro Alerts

Pomodoro alerts now work even if your new tab isn't open, so you won't miss the end of a focus session.

Shared Pomodoro Timer

Your focus timer now stays in sync across new tabs and keeps going after refreshes.

Automatic Weather

Weather can now detect your approximate location automatically, with manual city entry still available if you prefer.

Weather Refresh Improvements

Weather updates are more reliable and refresh automatically in the background.
